VIRSA 2026

  • Saturday, June 27 2026, 6:00pm - 10:00pm
VIRSA 2026 features the talents of three virtuoso brothers: Manmohan Waris, Kamal Heer, and Sangtar!
Together, the Waris brothers create a vibrant family-friendly atmosphere that celebrates Punjabi heritage!
 
Whether you’re a longtime Punjabi music fan or simply looking for a unique cultural experience, VIRSA 2026 promises an unforgettable evening on a Saturday night! Don’t miss being part of the grand cultural event.
 
Proudly presented by the Northern Sports and Cultural Club.
 
Tickets available only at TicketsNorth.
